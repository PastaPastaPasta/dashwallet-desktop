Shared masternode

Two to eight people fund one masternode together and split its payouts by share. One person coordinates; everyone else pastes what they receive and copies back a reply. Nothing reaches the network until the coordinator sends the registration.

Start oneYou enter who takes part, each share and the masternode's settings, then send everyone the same invitation.
Join or continuePaste an invitation, locked terms, a signing request or a reply you received, or open the file someone sent you.
Shares
2 to 8. Each is at least 100 Dash, and together they make exactly 1,000 Dash.
Your coins
Stay in this wallet, reserved for the session (your share plus your part of the network fee), until the registration is sent. Only you see which coins they are.
Leaving
Anyone can dissolve the masternode alone at any time and everyone gets their share back. In the early period, whoever leaves alone pays a penalty to the others.

How can people leave?

Anyone can always dissolve the masternode alone, and every share goes back to its owner. An early period protects the group: whoever leaves alone during it pays a penalty to the others.
