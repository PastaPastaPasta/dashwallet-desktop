## 1. Facts at the pin

- **F1. The library can't meet D44.** platform-wallet's `masternode_withdraw` (`wallet/masternode_withdrawal.rs`)
  builds, signs, broadcasts and waits in one call. `MasternodeWithdrawalUnconfirmed` carries no transition ID, nonce or
  bytes.
- **F2. The engine has what it needs one layer down.**
  - Public: dpp `IdentityCreditWithdrawalTransition` (v1, optional `output_script`); dash-sdk `IdentityNonceFetcher`
    (proved), `BroadcastStateTransition::broadcast`, `Document::fetch_with_metadata` (proved); platform-wallet's
    `masternode_withdrawal_keys`, which takes the caller's payout script (`masternode_withdrawal.rs:158-201`).
  - Not reachable: the library's `select_owner_withdrawal_key` and `select_transfer_withdrawal_key` (`pub(crate)`,
    `wallet/identity/network/mod.rs:52-54`). They also ignore the disabled flag.
- **F3. Drive decides an owner-key withdrawal's destination at execution.**
  - With no `output_script` (`rs-drive/…/identity_credit_withdrawal/v0/transformer.rs:84-175`), Drive reads the
    identity's TRANSFER keys in descending key id (`RecentWithdrawalKeys`,
    `rs-drive/src/drive/identity/key/fetch/mod.rs:1006-1017`).
  - It takes the highest-id key; if that key is disabled, the highest non-disabled key among the next 5. It pays P2PKH
    for an ECDSA_HASH160 key and P2SH for a BIP13_SCRIPT_HASH key.
  - Refusals: no TRANSFER key → `MissingTransferKeyError`; all 6 disabled → `IdentityPublicKeyIsDisabledError`;
    another key type → `NoTransferKeyForCoreWithdrawalAvailableError`.
  - The document id is `generate_document_id_v0(withdrawals, identity, "withdrawal", nonce_be ‖ script)`; the
    document holds `amount`, `coreFeePerByte`, pooling, `outputScript` and a status. Withdrawal documents are never
    deleted (only amount locks are), and keys are disabled, never deleted.
- **F4. Nothing at the pin proves that a withdrawal transition executed.**
  - `wait_for_response` returns `ExecutionNotProved` for withdrawals (`rs-sdk/.../broadcast.rs:43-53`, `:391-404`).
  - The verifier has no withdrawal case (`rs-drive/src/verify/state_transition/
    verify_state_transition_was_executed_with_proof/v0`: `IdentityCreditWithdrawal(_) => false`). The proof covers only
    the identity's balance.
  - DAPI's lookup by hash is the node's own Tenderdash read, and a refusal it reports carries no proof.
- **F5. DP6-02 has the pieces** (`dw-engine/src/platform/withdrawal.rs`): the proved-read seam, Drive's amount rules
  and `document_id`, `takes()` (Drive's nonce rule), `READ_BACK_TRIES`, and the lease, grant and permit plumbing.
  DP6-02 D21: every CheckTx answer is unknown; a withdrawal counts as executed only from the document, and a later
  one signs at the unconfirmed nonce while Drive would take it, so at most one executes.
- **F6. MG-00 D42's payout watch does not exist.** `payout_checked_height` is always `None` (MG-03 D5,
  `masternodes/reads.rs:526`). The raw ProTx watch is MG-03b's.
- **F7. The vault before MG-13.** Owner keys signed only ProUpRegTx payloads the vault hashes itself (`owner.rs`).
  Generic digest signing is `KeyUse::Sign` through `Signer::sign_ecdsa`. Attachable roles: `owner`, `voting`, `payout`
  (`vault/masternode.rs`). dpp is a normal dependency; its `state-transition-signing` feature is dev-only.
- **F8. Drive applies Core's masternode list in the block that moves its core height.** `update_core_info` →
  `update_masternode_list` → `update_masternode_identities/v1` and `update_owner_withdrawal_address/v1` apply payout
  changes, disabling and re-enabling TRANSFER keys. Under v24 multi-payee every TRANSFER key is disabled unless a sole
  P2PKH recipient remains (`reconcile_owner_payout_keys`). A shared masternode has no owner identity
  (`create_owner_identity/v2`). `ResponseMetadata.core_chain_locked_height` is Drive's `last_committed_core_height()`,
  quorum-signed through `StateId`; the engine's own synced header tip is `dw-engine/src/sync.rs`'s `tip_height()`.
- **F9. Desktop storage and locks.** `identity_locks` is keyed by `(wallet, identity)`. `delete_wallet` clears every
  `WALLET_TABLES` table. `protx_ops` keeps a durable `sent_at` marker and never deletes a sent row (MG-04 D6). DP3-04
  rule 5: money never moves on data verified only through the trusted-quorum fallback (`DP3-04-trust-gate.md`, D6).

## 2. Flow in short

`withdrawal_quote` reads the evonode's identity (proved, rule 5), applies D4's check and returns what the wallet holds,
the claimable credits and the destination per key, or `checking{reason}`. A host asks for consent once the destination
is fixed; the grant is that consent. `withdraw_credits` waits (before the grant) for the check, then under the
app-wide identity lock looks once more, checks the open record, reads the nonce, signs through the vault's single-use
scope, writes the record durably, and only then sends the stored bytes. Every later step (the background recheck, an
explicit `recheck_withdrawal`, marks) works from that record and never re-signs its bytes; Try again signs a new
sibling at the same nonce (D15). The facade is `mn-engine.md` §2.5; this file records why it is shaped so.

- **Scripted Platform** (`withdrawal/tests.rs`): a `World` (identity, used nonces, heights, documents) with per-read

  through dpp's nonce validation, and Drive's execution with the window as it is at execution. Tests enumerate D6's
  moves and its compare-and-set conflicts (a mark racing a proof, two rechecks), D7's matches and non-matches per
  candidate kind and script mapping (same nonce and script with another amount is a proved non-match), D8's floor,
  n ≤ tip and identity-height terms and an unverified read with a huge height not raising the floor in any mode, D10's
  group rule (tip + 1 only per D10; a final `done` group whose nonce is still takeable refuses it), D4 (header tip
  above the chain-locked height with an outside ProUpRegTx at the tip reads `checking`; the waits; the re-read before
  signing), D12 under each mode on every gated read, and D16's clearing terms (a deleted or unloaded creating wallet,
  conflicted, confirmed and covered, a discard of a `signed` and a `not_sent` row, a refused discard, the backfill).
- **Concurrency and storage** (`dw-appdb/src/mn_withdrawals_tests.rs`, engine): two sessions on one app database racing
  one identity; the unique index alone with the lock mutated away; wallet deletion keeping both tables; the supersede
  and its sibling insert as one transaction.
- **Vault** (`dw-vault/tests/masternode_withdrawal.rs`): D3's list, and a second signature under one scope refused.
- **Fault sweep (required gate; DEC-998 code-review condition).** A fault point (`DW_FAULT=<point>`, debug fault
  builds) at every durable write and network step: dw-appdb's `mn_withdraw.{insert, sent_at, state, finalize,
  not_executed, supersede, mark, floor, note}` and `mn_barrier.{write, clear, discard, backfill}`, the engine's
  `mn_withdraw.{broadcast, rebroadcast, sign, read_documents, read_nonce, read_proof}`, each with a before and an after
  stage. After each fault the restart asserts: no transition at another nonce while an earlier group is undecided and
  its nonce takeable or above the tip; stored bytes unchanged; no `done` without a matching proved document; no
  `not_executed` without D8's four conditions; no barrier lost, and none left without its row after a discard.

## 4. Residuals

- A payout change mined after the header tip the final check saw, and applied before execution (D4). MG-00 D42's raw
  ProTx watch (F6, MG-03b) would also close it.
- dw-app and UI binding and copy are MG-10's (D14); dw-app forwards no new call or event.
- rs-sdk has no raw-bytes rebroadcast at the pin: stored bytes are decoded, checked to re-encode to themselves, then
  broadcast (`withdrawal/net.rs`, `broadcast`). Bytes that don't round-trip are not sent, and the answer is unknown.
- A barrier's `created_at` is local wall-clock seconds, compared with Platform's block time (`check.rs`, the Platform
  evidence term): clock skew between the device and Platform moves when that term can clear a barrier.
- Platform evidence for clearing a barrier is "passed rule 5 under the session's mode", so `Probe` and `Off` accept an
  identity read that wasn't SPV-verified.
- The background recheck emits no event: a host re-reads `withdrawals`. Invariant alarms (a final group whose nonce is
  still takeable, a late document for a `not_executed` record) are logged and kept on the runtime, with no facade read
  yet (MG-10).
- The quote's balance read is not rule-5-gated: it decides nothing, and signing re-reads the identity under the lock.
- `couldnt_confirm` after `READ_BACK_TRIES` counted undecidable rounds is a retry state, not a verdict: the group
  stays undecided and is read again on every recheck.
- Rounds lost to failed or unverified reads never reach `couldnt_confirm` (D8 (3)): under `Enforce` with SPV never
  holding the quorum keys, or with DAPI unreachable, an open record stays `sent` (Withdraw blocked) for as long as that
  lasts, and each pass ends `Unanswered` with nothing written. The counter is per pass, not persisted.
