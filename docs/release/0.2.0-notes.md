# Dash Wallet Desktop 0.2.0

A desktop Dash wallet for macOS, Windows and Linux with DashPay (usernames and contacts), shielded funds and
Platform addresses on one recovery phrase. 0.2 adds Platform payments, moves between your own balances, fiat
prices, and a way to move mixed coins into Shielded.

**This is an early release. No security review has been done. Use small amounts only.** The app says so on
first run.

## What is new in 0.2

- **Platform addresses:** a Platform receive address, sending to Platform addresses, and Pay from Platform.
- **Transfer:** one sheet moves money between your own balances: Dash wallet → Shielded, Shielded → Dash wallet,
  Dash wallet → Platform addresses ("Add from Dash wallet"), and Platform addresses → Dash wallet.
- **Move mixed coins:** from the CoinJoin page, move your mixed coins to your wallet or into Shielded. The app
  combines them when there are too many for one transaction.
- **Unfinished transfers:** a move into Shielded or to your Platform addresses that was interrupted can be finished
  from Tools ▸ Repair or from the card on Home. That includes several at once after a restore.
- **Fiat:** prices in your local currency on Home, Activity and Confirm. You can type amounts in fiat on Pay and
  Receive. Prices can be turned off in Settings, which also clears them.
- **Switch networks without restarting:** changing between mainnet and testnet in Settings now happens in the running
  window. The network you leave is locked.
- **Network upgrade notice:** when Dash Platform runs a newer protocol than this version understands, Home says so
  and offers to open the releases page. Nothing is blocked, and you can dismiss it.
- **Home and Activity:** one readable balance; empty Shielded and Platform rows are hidden. Activity says who, when
  and how much in plain words. Transaction details fold the technical part away and can open the transaction in your
  browser.
- **Pay:** never dead-ends. A contact, a username search and a payment link each lead somewhere, and a sent payment's
  receipt is not hidden behind a refusal.
- **DashPay:** a welcome with next steps after you register a username, and Share Username on My Profile with a large
  QR code.
- **Reliability:**
  - a rescan interrupted by quitting resumes at the next launch, and payments wait until it is back;
  - coins credited by a rescan, or past the address gap, are restored;
  - funding a username, a top-up, your Platform addresses or Shielded from more than 50 coins is refused before
    anything is signed, with a hint to combine coins first.
- **Looks:** checkboxes read correctly in dark mode, every page fits a 1024-pixel-wide window, and leftover developer
  wording is gone.

## Known limitations

- **No security review.** Do not keep more than you can afford to lose.
- **Shielded availability depends on the network's Platform protocol version.** Where the shielded pool is not
  active, the app shows "Shielded is not active on this network yet".
- **Mixing (CoinJoin) is experimental.**
- **A normal payment or a move to Shielded doesn't use coins that are mixing or mixed.** On a wallet that mixes,
  Pay can say the amount exceeds your balance while Home shows more. To spend fully mixed coins, open Pay ▸
  "Advanced: coins, fee and change" and choose "Fully mixed funds only (CoinJoin)", or move them from the CoinJoin
  page.
- **Coins mixed in Dash Core (dash-qt) show in your balance after you restore the same recovery phrase, but Dash
  Wallet does not yet count them as mixed.** Don't start mixing on a wallet restored from Dash Core that already
  holds mixed coins.
- **After you restart Dash Wallet, Activity shows only part of your Shielded history.** Your shielded balance is not
  affected.
- **Right after a move into Shielded, and while a move from Shielded to your Dash wallet is pending, the Shielded
  balance can show 0.00** for a short while.
- **While a shielded payment is still being confirmed, that wallet can't be closed** (Settings ▸ Wallets ▸ Close)
  **and you can't switch networks.** Quitting the app is fine.
- **If storage fails or the computer loses power right after a shielded send, a second payment from that wallet can
  fail visibly.** After a restart the app asks you to confirm before sending again.
- **After you move everything from your Platform addresses to your Dash wallet, a small remainder (about 0.003
  DASH) can stay behind.**
- **Restoring a wallet created in the iOS app has not yet been tested end to end.**
- **English only.**
- **Masternode screens** (governance and username voting) are behind Settings ▸ Show Masternodes, off by default.
- Not yet: Platform ↔ Shielded transfers, shielded-funded usernames, invitation creation, the username marketplace,
  auto-update (see `docs/PLAN-1.0.md`).
- Not notarized (macOS) and not code-signed (Windows): the first launch shows a warning.
- **macOS: upgrading from 0.1 has not yet been checked on a real Mac.** After you install 0.2 over 0.1, macOS may
  ask again for access to the Keychain, or a wallet kept without a passphrase may ask you to unlock it. Keep your
  recovery phrase at hand before upgrading.

## How 0.2 was tested

Automatically, on the commit this release is built from: the wallet engine's tests on Linux, Windows and macOS; every
screen in Chromium and WebKit on built-in test data, with accessibility checks; the Linux app on its real wallet
engine, opened on a new, empty wallet with no network connection and walked through unlock, Pay, Home, Activity and
Contacts; and `dash:` links opened on all three systems. The installers below were built from that same commit by the
release workflow, which also starts the app once on each system.

This release was not checked by hand on testnet before it was published.

## Install

- **macOS:** open the dmg, drag Dash Wallet to Applications, then right-click ▸ **Open**, or run
  `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Dash Wallet.app"`.
- **Windows:** run the installer; at SmartScreen choose **More info ▸ Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `chmod +x dash-wallet-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage && ./dash-wallet-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`, or
  `sudo apt install ./dash-wallet-0.2.0-linux-amd64.deb`.

Upgrading from 0.1: install over it. Your wallets and settings stay where they are.
