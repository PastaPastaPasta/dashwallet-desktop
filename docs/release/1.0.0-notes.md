A desktop Dash wallet for macOS, Windows and Linux: the DashPay wallet of the iOS and Android apps, on the desktop.
One recovery phrase holds your Dash wallet, your DashPay username and contacts, your Shielded balance and your
Platform addresses. It also has the Dash Core tools a desktop user needs (coin control, PSBT, signing messages,
opening a Dash Core `wallet.dat`), and the masternode owner's screens: governance voting, registration and key
changes.

**No security review has been done. Use small amounts only.** The app says so on first run.

## What is in 1.0

- **Wallets:**
  - Create a wallet, or restore one from a 12- or 24-word recovery phrase, from a backup file, or from a Dash Core
    `wallet.dat` (legacy Berkeley DB, encrypted or not).
  - Write down and verify the phrase. Keep several wallets, and choose which open at start.
  - When you restore, give a date or block height to start from. A restore from the first block says it will be slow.
- **Security:**
  - A passphrase lock, or the system's secret store, and auto-lock.
  - Unlock once: payments up to your spending limit need no passphrase for five minutes.
  - The lock screen hides the balance, history and names.
  - Screens that show keys keep themselves out of screen captures.
  - Contact payments and masternode withdrawals refuse when the Platform data behind them can't be verified.
- **Send and receive:**
  - Receive by address, QR code or `dash:` link. Pay to an address, a contact, a `dash:` link, a QR code read from an
    image or the screen, or a link dropped on the window.
  - InstantSend, coin control, fee choice and subtract-fee.
  - Advanced: an unsigned transaction (PSBT).
  - Warnings when a payment mixes CoinJoin and unmixed coins, or leaves a large amount to the fee.
- **Activity:**
  - History for the Dash wallet, Shielded and Platform addresses, with details for each row: fee, raw hex, and copy.
  - Labels and notes. Export CSV.
- **DashPay:**
  - Register a username (contested or not), set your profile and picture, and top up or withdraw your credits.
  - Contacts: search, requests, accept, pay a contact, history and notifications.
  - Claim an invitation from a link or pasted text.
- **Shielded:**
  - The Shielded balance and receive address.
  - Move from the Dash wallet to Shielded and back, send to a shielded address, and move from Shielded to your own
    Platform address (test networks; see below).
  - Max on each move. The balance says how current it is.
  - An idle wallet syncs only when the shielded pool has changed.
- **Platform addresses:**
  - The balance, activity, and "Move to Dash wallet".
  - Transfers between the Dash wallet, Shielded and Platform, and finishing an unfinished transfer.
- **CoinJoin:**
  - Mixing with start, stop and settings, and progress with rounds.
  - Denominations are promoted and demoted as in Dash Core after V24 (test networks; see below).
  - Coins mixed in Dash Core count as mixed after you restore the same phrase.
  - CoinJoin Recovery, and an explanation of how mixing works.
- **Masternodes and governance:**
  - Shown automatically when the wallet holds masternode keys or work (Settings ▸ Show Masternodes: On, Auto or Off).
  - My masternodes: masternodes whose keys are in this wallet and ones you track, with status and payouts.
  - Masternode keys: each secret is behind the passphrase.
  - Add a tracked masternode's voting key and vote with it.
  - Governance: proposals, voting with one or many masternodes, and creating a proposal.
  - Username voting on contested names.
  - Registering a masternode or evonode, and changing its keys or payout (test networks; see below).
  - Shared masternodes (v24): start, join, approve, sign and send (test networks; see below).
- **Tools:**
  - Sign and verify messages, and the console.
  - Information and Sync progress, with each phase.
  - Repair: rescan, reset chain data, and remove unconfirmed transactions.
- **Settings:**
  - The network (mainnet or testnet), local currency, the minimum decimal places shown, and language.
  - Backups: automatic, Choose File, and Show Folder.
  - About: version, license, source code, where to report a problem, and updates.
- **Updates:** Settings ▸ About ▸ Updates checks for a new version, by hand or automatically (off by default). An
  update installs only when you choose to, and only after its signature is checked.
- **Languages:** English, plus the languages below, translated by machine (see Known limitations).

### Off on mainnet in 1.0

These work on testnet (and on regtest and devnet), and the app doesn't offer them on mainnet until each has had its
own funds review. On mainnet the app says they are available on test networks only, or doesn't show them.

- Registering a masternode or evonode, and changing its keys or payout (ProRegTx and ProUpRegTx sends).
- Shared masternodes: starting, joining, approving, signing and sending a shared registration, a reward change or a
  key change. A shared masternode you're part of is still listed, and its way-out steps (discard, abandon) still run.
- Paying a contact from the Platform balance.
- Topping up a username's credits from the Platform balance.
- Registering a username paid from your Platform addresses.
- Moving from Shielded to your own Platform address.
- Paying several recipients in one payment.
- CoinJoin promoting and demoting denominations (V24 conversions). Mixing itself works on mainnet.

Sends from your Platform addresses (Move to Dash wallet, and sending from Platform) are not behind an app setting:
they become available when Dash Platform enables address funds on the network, with no app update. Until then the
app shows "Platform addresses are not active on this network yet".

Shared-masternode sends stay off on mainnet until two recovery fixes land: a safe retry after an interrupted Abandon,
and a Send again after a database restore that can't hand off a costlier transaction than it charges.

## Known limitations

- **No security review.** Do not keep more than you can afford to lose.
- **Not notarized on macOS and not code-signed on Windows.** The first launch shows a warning; see Install.
  `SHA256SUMS` and every file are attested by GitHub's build provenance.
  <!-- RC: if the release key exists at the tag, add "SHA256SUMS is signed with the release key" and the Verify step
  for it (CHECKLIST §6). -->
- **The translations are made by machine** and checked by a second, independent machine pass, not by native
  speakers. Corrections are welcome as pull requests. Text that comes from the network or the wallet engine as a
  message, rather than a code, stays English, and so do a few system dialog titles.
- **Mixing (CoinJoin) is experimental.** A normal payment or a move to Shielded doesn't use coins that are mixing or
  mixed. To spend fully mixed coins, open Pay ▸ "Advanced: coins, fee and change" and choose "Fully mixed funds only
  (CoinJoin)". While mixing, the total on the CoinJoin page can move as sessions hold coins; Home's balance is right.
- **Shielded** depends on the network's Platform protocol version. Where the shielded pool isn't active, the app
  shows "Shielded is not active on this network yet". Home's history lists only Dash wallet rows; Shielded and
  Platform payments are in Activity.
- **Payments that never confirm:** if a payment can never confirm and the wallet can't prove which coins its
  replacement spent, those coins stay out of the balance. Removing the wallet and restoring it from its recovery
  phrase brings back any that are unspent.
  <!-- RC: the funds ruling keeps this for 1.0: the RC shows the held amount and this way out (MG's line); the
  proof-based release comes in 1.0. Match the wording to the app's line at the tag; drop it once the release ships. -->
- **Large wallets:** a wallet with tens of thousands of transactions takes several seconds to open, and its history
  pages load slowly.
- **Crashes while sending:** if the app quits while a payment is being sent, the payment stays pending and keeps its
  coins until it is mined or abandoned. Check that it went through before you send it again.
- **Restoring a wallet created in the iOS or Android app:** <!-- RC: say "tested on testnet" with what was checked,
  or keep this --> checked by comparing the derivation code; not yet tested end to end with a funded mobile wallet.
- **Flatpak:** a wallet needs a passphrase (the sandbox can't reach the system secret store), and the Flatpak keeps
  its data in its own folder, so it doesn't see the wallets of an AppImage or .deb install.
- **Not in 1.0:** creating invitations, the username marketplace, DashConnect, Explore and other partner services,
  Tor, Windows Hello and Touch ID unlock, and the tray icon on Windows and Linux.

## Install

- **macOS:** open the dmg and drag Dash Wallet to Applications. macOS says it can't check the app for malicious
  software. Right-click the app ▸ **Open**, then **Open** again, or run
  `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Dash Wallet.app"`.
- **Windows:** run the installer. At "Windows protected your PC" choose **More info ▸ Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** `chmod +x dash-wallet-1.0.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage && ./dash-wallet-1.0.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`,
  `sudo apt install ./dash-wallet-1.0.0-linux-amd64.deb`, or
  `flatpak install --user ./dash-wallet-1.0.0-linux-x86_64.flatpak` and then `flatpak run org.dash.wallet.desktop`.

## Upgrading

Install 1.0 over your current version. Your wallets, settings, labels and contacts stay where they are. Keep your
recovery phrase at hand before you upgrade, as with any wallet upgrade.

- **From 0.3.0 or later:** Settings ▸ About ▸ Check for updates offers 1.0 once it is published. Copies that keep the
  data in a Flatpak or a .deb update through Flatpak or apt; Settings says how.
- **From 0.1.x or 0.2.0:** these versions can't update themselves. Install 1.0 by hand, as above.
- **From a development build** (`0.x.y-dev.N`, from the `continuous` release or a per-commit build): development
  builds don't update themselves. Install 1.0 by hand. It opens the same data folder. If you used a development build
  on mainnet, check your balance and history after the first start, and run Tools ▸ Repair ▸ Rescan if anything looks
  wrong.
- **From a release candidate** (`1.0.0-rc.N`): install 1.0 by hand; candidates aren't offered updates.
- **From 0.1:** "Include my username in payment requests" (Settings ▸ Privacy) reads off. Turn it back on if you
  want it.
- **Going back to an earlier version after 1.0 has started isn't supported.** Before you upgrade, make a backup in
  Settings ▸ Backups if you might need to.
- **The earlier macOS app (the Swift one):** 1.0 doesn't read its data. To move a wallet from it, restore its
  recovery phrase in 1.0.
  <!-- RC: confirm against docs/qa/1.0-rc.md's "old Swift app's data" row before tagging. -->
- **macOS:** after you install 1.0 over an earlier version, macOS may ask again for access to the Keychain, or a
  wallet kept without a passphrase may ask you to unlock it once.

## Verify

Every file above and `SHA256SUMS` are attested by GitHub's build provenance: built by this repository's release
workflow from the tagged commit. The updates the app installs are also signed with the update key built into the
app, which it checks before installing.

```sh
sha=$(gh api repos/PastaPastaPasta/dashwallet-desktop/commits/v1.0.0 --jq .sha)
gh attestation verify dash-wallet-1.0.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage --repo PastaPastaPasta/dashwallet-desktop \
  --signer-workflow PastaPastaPasta/dashwallet-desktop/.github/workflows/release.yml \
  --source-ref refs/tags/v1.0.0 --source-digest "$sha"
gh attestation verify SHA256SUMS --repo PastaPastaPasta/dashwallet-desktop
sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS      # Linux; on macOS: grep <file name> SHA256SUMS | shasum -a 256 -c
```

For a release candidate, use its tag (`v1.0.0-rc.N`) and its file names. See `docs/release/verifying.md` in the
repository for the details.
